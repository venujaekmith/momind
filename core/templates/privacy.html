Privacy notice

MoMind stores sensitive maternal-health information. Only enter real patient data when your deployment has appropriate consent, access controls, backups, and legal approval.

Information used

The service may store account details, pregnancy and postpartum records, appointments, forum activity, uploaded documents, wellness check-ins, and messages you choose to submit.

How information is used

Information is used to provide role-based dashboards, care coordination, reminders, community features, and optional AI-assisted guidance. AI output is informational and is not a medical diagnosis.

Your choices

Use anonymous forum posting when appropriate, avoid sharing unnecessary identifiers, and contact the organization operating your MoMind deployment to request access, correction, export, or deletion.

Emergencies

Do not rely on this service for emergencies. Contact qualified medical professionals or local emergency services.
